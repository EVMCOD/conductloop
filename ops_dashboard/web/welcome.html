Welcome to ConductLoop

Your computer becomes a private AI second brain. Your notes, your code and your numbers stay on your machine — and you can ask it questions from your phone.

  • It reads your notes and answers from them, with a link to the note each answer used.
  • A team of agents works on your projects while you get on with something else.
  • Nothing leaves your computer: no account, no cloud, no subscription, ever.

Sample data is fictional, and it is removed the moment you index your own notes.

Two quick things and you are set

Both are optional, and everything stays on this computer.

1 · Your notes

ConductLoop reads your notes to answer from them. It never writes into your notes folder: its own notes (daily briefing, agent notes) go to a folder of their own.

2 · AI key (optional)

Search and notes work without it. To chat and summarise, paste a key from your AI provider. It is stored in the system keychain (the Keychain on a Mac), or in an encrypted file if there is none, and it is never shown again.

This computer has no system keychain available. Choose a passphrase so the key is stored in an encrypted file. You will be asked for it again next time you open the app.
