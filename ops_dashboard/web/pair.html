📱 Pair a phone

Generate a one-time code, scan it from the ConductLoop iOS app. The phone then connects to this machine over your network. Your data never leaves this device.

⚠ Mobile access requires the server bound to your LAN. Start it with OPS_ALLOW_MOBILE=1. Otherwise the phone can't reach it.

🌐 Remote access (outside your Wi-Fi): install Tailscale on this computer and your phone (same account). The QR already includes your Tailscale address, so the app connects from anywhere — no ports, no cloud.
