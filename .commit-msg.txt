feat(connectors): say when a credential expires, before it breaks

"Connect once" is only true for a personal token. An OAuth credential
expires on the provider's schedule: Slack and Microsoft with PKCE expire
after 30 days of inactivity, Google in *Testing* mode after 7, and a Gmail
refresh token stops working when the user changes their password.

The app cannot change that. What it can do is tell the truth instead of
failing later: the connector card says when the credential stops working,
and the app warns before it happens rather than the day the first sync
breaks.

The rules:

- The credential's OWN record is the truth. An OAuth credential carries its
  expiry and that is used. Only when the record says nothing does the
  connector's declared lifetime come in, and that is a provider rule (Slack:
  30 days of inactivity), not a guess.
- A personal token is never given an expiry. It lasts until the user
  revokes it, and inventing a date for it would be a lie. The card says so,
  which is what distinguishes a token from a consent at a glance.
- The check is cheap: it reads the stored record and never calls the
  provider. A page load must not turn into a request per connector, and a
  check must not be a side effect.
- The report names the connector and how many days are left, and it never
  carries the credential itself.
